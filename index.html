Market Brief

A daily India stock market report in Hinglish, published on YouTube.

Every trading evening, after the Indian market closes, Market Brief publishes a short video covering the market's health, Nifty and Bank Nifty trends, FII and DII flows, global markets, sectors, top movers and the stocks a quantitative model ranked highest that day.

The video is generated automatically from public end-of-day market data and uploaded to our own YouTube channel.
